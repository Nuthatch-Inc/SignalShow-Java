Cepstrum

SignalShow computes the magnitude cepstrum:

IFFT(log(abs(FFT(input))))

The forward transform is unnormalized and the inverse transform divides by the number of samples (width times height for images). The input may have both real and imaginary channels. For a complex input, the magnitude cepstrum can also have an imaginary channel; SignalShow preserves it.

The logarithm uses the spectrum's magnitude and discards its phase. This is not a complex cepstrum with unwrapped spectral phase. Exactly zero spectral bins use a finite logarithm value of -100.

Selecting inverse computes IFFT(exp(FFT(input))). Applied after the magnitude cepstrum, this reconstructs the original spectrum's magnitude with zero spectral phase. It cannot restore the phase discarded by the forward operation.
