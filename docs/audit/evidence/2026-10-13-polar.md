PolarFunctionMath.reflectZeroStart and Cartesian generation use height−1−y.

used different positions. Generate each angle from x=column,y=height−1−row;
retain the existing90° choice at the undefined polar origin. Centered indexing
is unchanged. This angle buffer's only production consumer is PolarFunctionTerm2D.
